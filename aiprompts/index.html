CHARACTER RECAST / SOURCE-ACTOR TRACKING

Use the uploaded SOURCE VIDEO as the absolute master reference for motion, choreography, timing, camera movement, framing, lens perspective, depth, occlusion, environment and shot progression.

This is a MAIN-SUBJECT IDENTITY REPLACEMENT task. Preserve the existing guard cast and scene.

SOURCE-SPECIFIC PERFORMANCE MAP — AMMAR'S SUPPLIED VIDEO

The uploaded source is already a generated scene. Use THIS uploaded video as the only motion and scene reference; no other original clip is required.

Observed sequence (approximate landmarks for orientation, never replacement keyframes or new timing):
- Opening: a tight front-facing view of the white-shirt performer with a cigarette held near the mouth in the right hand. Smoke is visible as the head lowers and turns. The guards behind are softer in focus.
- Early section: the same performer turns away from camera, showing the back of the white shirt, then moves deeper between the guard rows. Preserve the exact route, shoulder turns, arm lifts, hand gestures and changing occlusions.
- Middle section: the performer turns back toward camera within the formation, lowers the head, raises the cigarette hand toward the mouth again, then lowers the hand. Preserve the source's precise hand-to-mouth contact, pauses and head movement.
- Later section: the white-shirt performer remains comparatively upright while the surrounding guards begin their source-specific arm/lapel gestures, head rolls, leans and deep forward bows. The guards do not all occupy the same phase at once. Preserve each person's individual timing.
- Ending: the view has widened to show the performer, multiple rows of moving guards, stone steps, the large brick archway, pale stone trim and dark vertical panels. Retain the source's ending pose and frame; do not invent a new ending.

These descriptions help identify the source performance. The actual uploaded video is authoritative for every frame, movement, onset, duration, direction, camera change and detail. Do not substitute an approximation based on this text.

CIGARETTE / HAND / SMOKE CONTINUITY:
The cigarette is a performance prop, not part of the person's identity. Preserve it through the identity replacement.
Match the source hand (right hand where visible), finger grip, cigarette position, angle, scale, hand-to-mouth movement, mouth contact, lowering of the hand and subsequent visible holding position.
Preserve visible smoke at the source moments, with the same location, direction, density and dissipation as closely as possible.
Do not omit the cigarette, swap it between hands, turn it into another object, add a second cigarette, add extra smoking gestures or make it float independently of the hand.
When the hand or cigarette is occluded or outside the frame, preserve that visibility state; do not expose it for clarity or invent hidden action.
Maintain anatomically coherent fingers, lips and contact throughout.

CAMERA / ENVIRONMENT / LIGHTING:
Preserve the continuous widening from the close opening to the broad view of the formation and architecture. Match the actual source trajectory, rate of pull-back, perspective, focus changes and subject scale; do not invent a dolly, orbit, cut or zoom beyond what the source shows.
Keep the stair geometry, rows, brick arch, pale trim, dark panels, lighting direction, muted color palette, contact shadows and source depth of field. Do not replace the setting with a studio or the white image-reference background.
