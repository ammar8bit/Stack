About StackTime

StackTime helps students build consistent study habits. Track a full study session, time individual practice questions, or run a custom countdown while your total study time keeps moving forward.

Stay on pace

Set a total-hours target and deadline to see the study time you need each day.

Practice with intent

Time individual questions while keeping your wider study session running.

Private by default

No account is required. Your settings and progress stay in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

Is StackTime free?

Yes. StackTime is free to use and does not require an account or subscription.

Who is StackTime for?

It is designed for students preparing for exams, certifications, coursework, and other long-term study goals.

Where is my study progress stored?

Your timer settings and progress are stored locally in your browser, so they are not sent to a server.
